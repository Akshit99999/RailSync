[ MODULE 1: REAL-TIME GPS TELEMETRY & ROLLING STOCK]
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F01 | High-Throughput GPS Stream Ingestion Pipeline
    - Purpose: Ingests high-frequency GPS telemetry packets from locomotive RTIS devices without packet drops.
    - Stack: Spring Cloud Stream, Apache Kafka, Spring WebFlux, Netty
    - Component: LiveTracker.js (Telemetry HUD), TrackMap.js (Real-time marker updates)
    - Implementation: Decodes binary/JSON telemetry payloads, validates hardware signatures, checks timestamp monotonicity, writes to partitioned Kafka topic 'train-telemetry-events'.
    - Priority: P0 | Complexity: High

F02 | Spatial Corridor Map Matching & Distance-to-Go
    - Purpose: Snaps raw, jittery GPS coordinates onto official IR track polylines and computes remaining distance to upcoming stations.
    - Stack: Hibernate Spatial, PostGIS, JTS Topology Suite
    - Component: RouteRibbon.js (Permanent way sleeper pips), TrackMap.js (Corridor polyline)
    - Implementation: Projects point onto line segments using ST_ClosestPoint and ST_LineLocatePoint across 68,000+ km of GIS track database, eliminating off-track GPS noise.
    - Priority: P0 | Complexity: High

F03 | Reactive WebSocket / SSE Telemetry Broadcaster
    - Purpose: Replaces client-side 30s interval polling with low-latency reactive server-push streams.
    - Stack: Spring WebSocket, STOMP Protocol, Project Reactor (Flux/SSE)
    - Component: LiveTracker.js (Auto-refresh loop), Navbar.js (Live clock & sync)
    - Implementation: Establishes persistent STOMP/WebSocket channels per train (/topic/train/{trainNumber}). Broadcasts telemetry packets on GPS ingestion with sub-second latency.
    - Priority: P0 | Complexity: Medium

F04 | Locomotive Dynamic Bearing & Smoothing Engine
    - Purpose: Calculates smooth navigational heading azimuth between sequential GPS pings to orient locomotive marker realistically.
    - Stack: Apache Commons Math (Kalman Filter), Java Math
    - Component: TrackMap.js (Directional locomotive rotation), LiveTracker.js (Compass HUD)
    - Implementation: Executes Haversine forward azimuth calculations combined with 1D Kalman filtering to prevent visual jitter when negotiating curves or stopping at signals.
    - Priority: P1 | Complexity: Medium

F05 | Train Rake Composition & Rolling Stock Registry
    - Purpose: Maintains physical locomotive and coach sequences from engine to guard van with class specifications.
    - Stack: Spring Data JPA, PostgreSQL, Lombok, Jackson GeoJSON
    - Component: CoachLayout.js (Train rake diagram), PnrSection.js (Coach highlight)
    - Implementation: Relational model representing rakes, coach sequences (WAP-7, EOG, 3A, 2A, 1A, PC, SL, SLR), tare weights, coach classes, and reverse-rake configurations for terminus stations.
    - Priority: P1 | Complexity: Medium

SECTION 5: COMPLETE CLASS, INTERFACE, ENUM & RECORD DICTIONARY
====================================================================================================

[DOMAIN ENTITIES & MODELS]
- Train: Core aggregate representing a scheduled train run across the IR network.
- Rake: Physical rake assembly comprising a locomotive, power cars (EOG), passenger coaches, and pantry car.
- Coach: Individual rail car with defined coach class, tare weight, and berth layout formula.
- Locomotive: Traction unit (e.g. WAP-7, WAG-9, WDP-4D) fitted with RTIS GPS hardware.
- TimetableHalt: Ordered station stop with scheduled arrival/departure, day rollover, and platform.
- Station: Physical railway station with GIS coordinates, Hindi/English names, and platform tracks.
- Platform: Station platform track with physical length, electrification status, and occupancy lock.
- RailwayZone: Top-level administrative zone (e.g. NR, WR, CR, SR, ER).
- OperatingDivision: Operational division (e.g. Delhi Division, Mumbai Central Division).
- PnrRecord: 10-digit Modulus-11 validated passenger booking containing travel itinerary and passengers.
- Passenger: Individual traveler holding waitlist or confirmed berth allocation.
- BerthAllocation: Physical berth location (Coach Number, Berth Number, Berth Type, Window Indicator).
- LiveTelemetry: Snapshot of real-time train movement (Lat/Lng, speed, smoothed heading, distance-to-go, signal).

[RECORDS]
- GpsTelemetryPacket: Immutable telemetry ingestion DTO with cryptographic signature and monotonicity check.
- SnappedResult: Output of GIS PostGIS map-matching containing projected point and deviation distance.
- SplitFlapStationBoard: Aggregated snapshot of rolling arrival/departure station board movements.
- DelayPropagationReport: Downstream bottleneck impact report generated by DAG graph traversal.

[ENUMS]
- CoachClass: 1A (First AC), 2A (Two Tier AC), 3A (Three Tier AC), 3E (3AC Economy), SL (Sleeper), CC (Chair Car), 2S (Second Sitting).
- BerthType: LOWER, MIDDLE, UPPER, SIDE_LOWER, SIDE_MIDDLE, SIDE_UPPER, WINDOW_SEAT, AISLE_SEAT.
- SignalAspect: GREEN (Delay <= 5 mins), AMBER (5 < Delay <= 25 mins), RED (Delay > 25 mins or Cancelled).
- ChartStatus: NOT_PREPARED, CHART_PREPARED.
- BookingStatus: CONFIRMED, RAC, GNWL, PQWL, RLWL, CANCELLED.
- ChannelType: SMS, WHATSAPP, WEB_PUSH, IVR_VOICE.
- Tier: FREE, PRO, ENTERPRISE.
